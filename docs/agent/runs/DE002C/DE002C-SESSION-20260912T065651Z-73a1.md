## Scope completed

R1 legal composition (retained) plus R2 correctness:

- Artifact uniqueness is `(workflow_id, sha256, artifact_type, version)`. SHA256 remains the real content digest and is not salted with workflow identity.
- Pinned contract compatibility is evaluated on a runnable candidate before `claim_task`. `CONTRACT_VERSION_UNAVAILABLE` parks the task, blocks the workflow, and creates no TaskAttempt, lease, or recovery owner.
- Tool Registry policy runs before dispatch. `on_policy_allow` records external dispatch only after ALLOW. ENVIRONMENTAL PARK and SCOPE/SECURITY FAIL_CLOSED leave `dispatch_state=NOT_DISPATCHED`, a terminal FAILED attempt, and no `active_attempt_id` / lease / recovery owner.

- Planning templates receive server `effective_grants`. `validate_plan` rejects a proposed task whose required permission is not in that set. Tool Registry remains the non-bypassable execution guarantee.
- Two sequential identical offline workflows in one PostgreSQL database both COMPLETED and retain their own RequirementSpec, Plan, ValidationReport, and WorkflowSummary rows. A concurrent pair also COMPLETED.
